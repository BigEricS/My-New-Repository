# SDM — AI SEO Outreach Hooks & Offer Positioning

## The Differentiation Angle
Most SEO agencies across the US only cover Google rankings.
SDM covers both Google SEO AND AI search visibility (ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity, Google AI Overviews).
No traditional agency offers this at scale — it's a national first-mover opportunity.

## Target Markets (National)
Go niche-first, not geo-first. Pick 2-3 verticals and own them nationally:
- **Home services** (roofing, HVAC, plumbing, landscaping) — high local search volume, low AI optimization
- **Legal** (personal injury, family law, estate) — high CPCs, agencies overcharge, AI search growing fast
- **Healthcare / MedSpas** — E-E-A-T is critical, most are behind on AI search
- **E-commerce** — product schema + AI citation = untapped competitive edge
- **Real estate agents / brokerages** — AI search is disrupting how buyers find agents
