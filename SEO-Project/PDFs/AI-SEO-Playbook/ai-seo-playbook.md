## The 7 Parallel Agents

One command spins up 7 parallel AI subagents to crawl your entire site simultaneously.

Your URL → 7 Agents:

1. **Technical SEO** — Crawls structure, robots.txt, page speed
2. **On-Page Analysis** — Reviews title tags, H1s, keyword density
3. **Content Quality** — E-E-A-T scoring, readability
4. **Schema Markup** — Checks JSON-LD data
5. **Image Optimization** — Alt text, lazy loading
6. **AI Search (GEO)** — Citability, brand mentions
7. **Strategic Planning** — Competitor gaps, action priorities
